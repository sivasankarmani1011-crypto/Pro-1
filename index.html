About Our Shop

For over 15 years, Arun Ice Cream & Grocery has been a part of everyday life in Kallal. What began as a small neighbourhood shop is now a trusted place for families to pick up cool treats and daily essentials.

Our day starts early. We open at 5:30 AM so you can get fresh milk and groceries before work or school, and we stay open until 10:00 PM for your late-evening ice cream.
